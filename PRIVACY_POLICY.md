# Privacy Policy for TaskFlow

**Last Updated:** September 22, 2026

## Overview

TaskFlow is a local-first task management application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how TaskFlow handles your data.

## Data Collection

**TaskFlow does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.**

- ✅ No analytics or tracking
- ✅ No cookies or advertising identifiers
- ✅ No user behavior monitoring
- ✅ No third-party services or SDKs that collect data
- ✅ No internet connection required for core functionality

## Data Storage

All your data — including tasks, user accounts, preferences, and settings — is stored **exclusively on your device** using the browser's `localStorage` API (on web) or the app's local storage (on Android).

- Your data never leaves your device
- No cloud sync or server-side storage
- No backups are sent to external servers
- Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data

## Permissions

TaskFlow may request the following device permissions:

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Notifications** | To deliver task reminder alerts you schedule |
| **Internet** | Only used for loading web fonts on first launch |
| **Boot Completed** | To restore scheduled reminders after device restart |
| **Wake Lock** | To ensure reminders fire at the scheduled time |
| **Vibrate** | For haptic feedback on notifications |

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit your personal data.

## Third-Party Services

TaskFlow does **not** integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

## Children's Privacy

TaskFlow does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app does not collect any personal information from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, the changes will be reflected in this document with an updated "Last Updated" date.
